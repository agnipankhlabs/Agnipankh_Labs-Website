## 1. Executive Summary

### Overview
**Agnipankh Labs** is an innovative EdTech and career enablement startup headquartered in Maharashtra, India. Dedicated to bridging the growing divide between traditional academic education and modern industry demands, Agnipankh Labs delivers a comprehensive experiential learning ecosystem. Our platform offers structured internships, hands-on technical training, project-based capstone simulations, cryptographically verifiable professional certifications, and personalized mentorship.

### Problem Statement
Over 80% of engineering and higher-education graduates in emerging markets face severe employability challenges. Academic curricula often focus heavily on theoretical concepts, leaving students without practical hands-on experience in modern technology stacks, real-world collaborative workflows, or professional delivery standards. Conversely, recruiters spend significant resources upskilling entry-level talent.

### The Solution
Agnipankh Labs offers a multi-modal, tech-driven platform integrating six core pillars:
1. **Structured Internship Programs:** 5-stage application pipeline with real project tasks across high-growth domains (Web Dev, Data Science, AI/ML, Cybersecurity, HR, Marketing).
2. **Industry-Aligned Training Programs:** Modular skill-building courses with interactive video, reading, code labs, and assessments.
3. **Tamper-Evident Certifications:** FROZEN certificate identity system utilizing non-sequential Crockford Base32 identifiers, SHA-256 cryptographic hashes, and instant public verification via QR code (`/verify/[certificateId]`).
4. **Live Capstone Projects:** Real workplace simulations with sprint reviews and collaborative Git workflows.
5. **Mentorship & Evaluation:** Weighted 8-criteria performance rubrics and direct guidance from industry mentors.
6. **Career & Placement Support:** Resume reviews, interview readiness, and portfolio acceleration.

### Business Model
Agnipankh Labs operates a hybrid B2C and B2B revenue model:
- **B2C Revenue:** Paid training programs, specialized certification tracks, premium mentorship packages, and candidate program fees (where applicable).
- **B2B / B2G Revenue:** Institutional campus partnerships (MoU-based student training), corporate candidate sourcing fees, sponsored hackathons/events, and CSR-funded upskilling initiatives.

### Current Stage & Execution Progress
- **Technical Platform:** Next.js 15 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Prisma ORM 7 with a comprehensive 40+ model PostgreSQL schema, Auth.js v5 (NextAuth), RBAC, TOTP-based Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Upstash Redis rate limiting, Resend email system, PDFKit certificate generator, and an integrated Admin CRM/Finance/Analytics suite.
- **Development Status:** Core web platform, LMS engine, certificate verification, application pipelines, and admin ops modules are fully coded (~95% code completion). Database migration and cloud hosting provisioning are currently in progress prior to public commercial launch.
- **Traction & Metrics:** Pre-launch stage. No live users or revenue claimed to date; Year 1 targets include 1,000+ enrolled learners and 25+ college MoUs.

### Growth Opportunity & Vision
Agnipankh Labs is positioned to capture a significant share of the rapidly growing Indian EdTech and vocational skill-building market (projected to reach $10B+). Over a 3–5 year horizon, Agnipankh Labs aims to scale into a national innovation hub, launching a proprietary AI learning recommendation engine, a research wing, and a early-stage startup incubation accelerator.

## 6. Problem Statement

### 1. Theoretical Academia vs. Practical Industry Requirements
- **Who Experiences It:** University students and recent graduates in CS, IT, Engineering, and Business disciplines.
- **Why It Happens:** College curricula update slowly, often lagging behind rapidly evolving technology stacks (e.g., Next.js, Cloud Architectures, AI/ML Tooling).
- **Existing Limitations:** Traditional lectures emphasize examinations over project builds, code reviews, and production deployments.
- **Impact:** Graduates lack practical portfolio proof and struggle during technical job interviews.

### 2. High Candidate Screening & Retraining Costs for Employers
- **Who Experiences It:** IT companies, startups, and corporate recruiters.
- **Why It Happens:** High volume of applicant resumes with unverified skill claims; lack of standardized project validation.
- **Existing Limitations:** Hiring teams must invest 3–6 months retraining entry-level hires in modern workplace tools and agile workflows.
- **Impact:** Increased time-to-hire, high training expenditure, and low immediate workforce productivity.

### 3. Certificate Fraud and Lack of Verification Mechanics
- **Who Experiences It:** Employers, certification bodies, and honest job seekers.
- **Why It Happens:** Paper and PDF certificates are easily fabricated using graphical editing tools.
- **Existing Limitations:** Employers lack quick, reliable methods to verify if a candidate completed genuine project work.
- **Impact:** Devaluation of authentic accomplishments and reputational risk for educational institutions.

### 4. Limited Access to Quality Mentorship in Tier-2/3 Regions
- **Who Experiences It:** Students outside major metropolitan technology hubs.
- **Why It Happens:** Geographic centralization of technology companies and expert networks in tier-1 cities.
- **Existing Limitations:** High cost of private career coaching and lack of local industry connections.
- **Impact:** Talent in smaller cities remains under-served and under-represented in top technology roles.

### How Agnipankh Labs Addresses These Problems
Agnipankh Labs provides a digital platform with live workplace simulations, instant cryptographic certificate validation, standardized 8-point mentor performance evaluations, and direct access to industry experts regardless of geographic location.

## 7. Proposed Solution

### Core Solution Overview
Agnipankh Labs is a unified web-based EdTech and career enablement ecosystem (`agnipankhlabs.com`). It combines an Internship Management System, a Learning Management System (LMS), an Automated Certificate Verification Platform, and an Administrative Operations CRM into a single seamless portal.

### Major Platform Capabilities
- **5-Stage Internship Pipeline:** Guided candidate selection (Application Received → Eligibility Check → Selection Decision → Offer Letter Issued → Joining Confirmation).
- **Interactive LMS:** Structured course delivery with ordinal lessons, video streaming, reading materials, code labs, and pass-gated quiz engines (70% passing threshold).
- **Cryptographic Certificate Verification:** Permanently immutable certificate system issuing non-sequential Crockford Base32 IDs with SHA-256 integrity verification, instant QR code rendering, and PDF generation.
- **Mentor Evaluation Engine:** Structured 8-criteria evaluations (Attendance, Communication, Technical Skills, Problem Solving, Teamwork, Learning Ability, Professionalism, Overall Performance).
- **Admin Operational Suite:** Integrated lead management CRM, SLA-tracked support ticketing system (24h/12h/6h SLAs), GST-compliant financial accounting, and mentor onboarding workflows.

### User & Business Benefits
- **For Students:** Verifiable portfolio projects, proof-of-work credentials, industry mentorship, and improved job placement readiness.
- **For Academic Institutions:** Turnkey internship programs, MoU partnerships, and verifiable student outcome data.
- **For Corporate Employers:** Pre-vetted, industry-ready talent pool with transparent project evaluation records.

## 19. Technology Stack

### Frontend Architecture
- **Framework:** Next.js 15.5 (App Router with Turbopack enabled)
- **UI & View Engine:** React 19.1, TypeScript 5
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 with custom `@theme` design tokens (Innovation Orange `#B35100` AA-compliant text token `brand-ink`, Navy `#0F172A`)
- **Component Primitives:** Class Variance Authority (`cva`), `clsx`, `tailwind-merge`, Lucide React icons
- **Data Visualization:** Chart.js `^4.5` (Analytics dashboard time-series & distribution charts)

### Backend & API Layer
- **Execution Architecture:** Next.js Server Actions & App Router Route Handlers (no external API servers required)
- **Validation Engine:** Zod `^4.5` schemas for all incoming form submissions, payloads, and URL parameters
- **Authentication:** Auth.js v5 (NextAuth `^5.0.0-beta.32`) with Credentials Provider, bcrypt password hashing, account lockout state, and TOTP Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) engine
- **Email Delivery Service:** Resend `^6.26` via server action triggers (fail-safe non-blocking background queue)

### Database & Storage Architecture
- **ORM:** Prisma ORM `7.10.0` with `@prisma/adapter-pg`
- **Database Engine:** PostgreSQL `8.23` (Comprehensive 40+ model schema, 1,638+ lines)
- **Rate Limiting & Cache:** Upstash Redis (`@upstash/redis` `^1.38`, `@upstash/ratelimit` `^2.0.8`) with fail-open security strategy
- **Media & File Storage:** `[PLANNED TECHNOLOGY — Cloudinary integration configured in environment variables, media client implementation planned post-database provisioning]`

### Certificate Crypto & PDF Engine
- **PDF Generation:** PDFKit `^0.20.2`
- **QR Code Engine:** QRCode `^1.5.4` generating inline Base64 / SVG data URIs
- **Hashing Security:** SHA-256 tamper-evident integrity string + Crockford Base32 non-sequential public ID format (`AL-{TYPE}{YY}-{10 CHARS}`)

### Quality & Build Tooling
- **Type Checking:** TypeScript strictly enforced (`tsc --noEmit`)
- **Linting:** ESLint 9 with `eslint-config-next`
- **Testing & Quality Scripts:** Vitest `^2.1.9`, custom contrast checker (`scripts/check-contrast.mjs`), certificate contract verification script (`scripts/check-certificates.ts`). Combined CI gate: `npm run check`.

## 21. Security & Privacy

### Currently Implemented Security Protocols
- **Authentication & Password Security:** Passwords hashed exclusively with bcrypt; plain text passwords are never stored. 5 failed login attempts trigger locked-account status (`lockedUntil`).
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Middleware checks active user roles against route policies (`/admin/*`, `/dashboard/*`). Staff roles and privileged access are strictly enforced.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Mandatory TOTP MFA engine configured for Super Admin and Finance roles as mandated by Cybersecurity Framework AL-SEC-001.
- **Certificate Integrity (FROZEN):** Non-sequential Crockford Base32 Certificate IDs with SHA-256 cryptographic signatures. Secrets cannot be rotated post-issuance without invalidating legacy certificates.
- **Financial Security:** Monetary figures stored strictly as integers in paise (1 INR = 100 paise) to prevent floating-point rounding errors. Payment credentials are handling via gateway tokens only.
- **Data Retention Metadata:** Every personal or financial data table carries DPDP Act 2023 retention class annotations (`PERMANENT`, `YEARS_7`, `YEARS_5`, `YEARS_3`, `YEARS_2`, `YEARS_1`).

### Privacy & Compliance Considerations
- **DPDP Act 2023 Alignment:** Platform includes explicit `Consent` tracking model with purpose attributes and withdrawal support.
- **Grievance Officer:** `[TO BE CONFIRMED — Founder to designate legal grievance officer post-incorporation]`.
